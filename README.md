# The Impact of Inventory Management Efficiency on Working Capital and Firm Performance

**MSc Supply Chain Management — MBS School of Business**  
**Academic Year:** 2025–2026  
**Author:** Jagadeeswar Reddy Kannam

## About the Dissertation

This research examines the relationship between inventory management efficiency, working capital efficiency, and firm performance in the retail and FMCG sector.

The study covers **15 companies from 2010 to 2025**, using **238 firm-year observations**. Inventory management efficiency is measured using **Inventory Turnover**, working capital efficiency using the **Quick Ratio**, and firm performance using **Tobin's Q**. The analysis uses correlation, regression, and mediation tests conducted in IBM SPSS Statistics.

## Research Focus

- Inventory Management Efficiency → Working Capital Efficiency
- Inventory Management Efficiency → Firm Performance
- Working Capital Efficiency → Firm Performance
- Mediating role of Working Capital Efficiency
